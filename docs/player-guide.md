# LOTR NPCs: player guide

Soldiers of every people of Middle-earth roam the land. Fight them, or join a faction and lead your own band.

## 1. Pick your faction

When you make your character, a menu asks which people you fight for: Free Peoples, Shadow or Others. Your faction's NPCs treat you as one of their own and you can hire them. Everyone else's soldiers attack you on sight (the Shire only defends itself). **The choice is permanent**; only an admin can change it. Closed the menu by mistake? Type `/recruits join`.

## 2. Hire soldiers

Right-click one of your faction's NPCs to talk, and choose **"I'd like to hire you."** You pay in **rusty gears** from your hotbar or backpack.

| Rank | Price | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recruit | 5 | Cheap fighter. Can be a plain, guard (tougher) or shock (harder-hitting) recruit. |
| Warrior | 15 | Solid line fighter. |
| Archer | 15 | Shoots from range, every faction but the Shire. |
| Captain | 40 | Strongest fighter. **One per player.** |

Every faction has the same numbers, so battles are won by tactics and numbers, not by which people you picked.

**Your band:** up to **3 followers** of any rank, plus **1 extra recruit**, and **16 recruits** in all. Anyone past that guards where you hired them. You can have only 1 captain.

## 3. Talk to your recruits

Right-click your own recruit:

- **Follow me / Hold your position here / Guard this area**: guards patrol 16 blocks round their post and attack intruders, even while you're offline.
- **Guard this area for our people**: members of your `/group` may borrow this guard ("Come with me").
- **Let me change how you look**: name, helmet, weapon, shield.
- **Let me change your class**: a recruit switches between plain, guard and shock recruit, for free.
- **Promote...**: appears once it has enough XP (below).
- **I don't need you any more**: dismiss (coins aren't refunded).

## 4. Give orders in battle

**Hold R** for the orders menu (rebind it in Settings > Controls). Click an order or press its number; scroll or Tab switches pages. It reaches your recruits within about 96 blocks.

- **Movement:** Follow me, Hold position, Move there (where you look), Guard here, Guard here for my group, and a **formation**: Loose, Line, Column, Wedge, Square or Circle.
- **Combat:** a **stance** (Passive: never fight; Defensive: only fight what attacks; Aggressive: attack every enemy in sight; Raid: attack every player and creature that isn't allied), **Retreat** (they run back to you and start no fights for 10 s) and **Charge** (they rush the enemy you look at, out of formation).

Tips: soldiers hit from behind take **x1.5 damage**, so flank. Followers in a formation form a battle line and wrap round a smaller enemy.

## 5. Promote

Your recruits and warriors earn **1 XP** for each enemy NPC that dies within 10 seconds of their hit. Look at a recruit to see its `XP: (x/y)`. When it's full you get a message; talk to it and choose **Promote...** (costs rusty gears).

- **Recruit, 15 XP:** warrior, guard, shock trooper or archer (12 gears each).
- **Warrior, guard or shock, 40 XP:** **captain** or **rider** (36 each).

A promoted soldier keeps its name, looks and orders, and puts on the new rank's armor.

**Riders** come on their own mount: horses for Men and most others, dire wolves for orcs and Uruk-hai, boars for dwarves, caribou for elves (the mount needs its mod on the server). Their charge hits do x2.5. Riders hunt archers and beat a couple of warriors, but lose to a captain.

## 6. Keep track

- `/recruits roster`: every recruit you have, wherever they are, with buttons to mark them on the map, call them, promote or dismiss them. Also in the orders menu.
- `/recruits whitelist`: players your recruits never attack on sight.
- `/recruits dismissall`: let everyone go.

Wounded soldiers heal on their own about 10 seconds after their last fight. A fallen soldier's body can be searched with a knife for a few rusty gears and cloth or leather. Armor and weapons aren't dropped.
