# Model checklist: what each faction still needs

Updated 2026-11-08 for 0.0.101 (started 2026-11-04 after 0.0.81). Owner: "make a list of the factions and weapons they
need to fill out their kits"; 2026-11-08: "give me a list of all the weapons needed ... for models".

**Since 0.0.101 the gaps are filled with the game's own weapons** (owner: "build it that way"): codes read from the
server's Combat Overhaul fork, which keeps the game's item codes. "Ruined" ones are the game's rusty finds. Each
stand-in goes as soon as your model for that slot arrives. Not yet seen in game: how each game item looks and sits in
an NPC's hand, and whether the codes exist on a server without Combat Overhaul.

How to read it: **★ = your preference** from the 0.0.82 loadout list; it comes up three times as often as the
faction's other weapons. Unmarked items are my picks by lore. Cross out anything you don't want. Tick an item when its
model is in the mod.

**Count:** 46 melee weapon models (plus optional wicked-dwarf versions), 15 bows and 4 shields if every faction gets its own (the shields are the three
marked "my note" plus Isengard's). Shared models cut that down a lot. One Mannish set (spear, sword, axe, dagger,
halberd) could serve Gondor, Rohan, Dale, Arnor and the Dunlendings, retextured per faction later, and one Mannish bow
the same five. My recommendation for the order: a Mannish spear and sword first (they cover the most ★ preferences:
Gondor, Dale, Arnor, Easterlings), then the Easterling saber and halberd, then daggers (nobody outside the orcs and
dwarves has one yet).

## When a model arrives

Send the .bbmodel (textures embedded) with the faction, the weapon, and whether it's one- or two-handed. It goes in
`tools/weapons/source/` and is converted at build time; gen `WEAPON_PREFS` gets the ★ weights back for that faction.
