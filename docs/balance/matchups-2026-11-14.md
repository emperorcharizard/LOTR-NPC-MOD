# Matchup analysis, 2026-11-14 (0.0.133 rules, the owner's ModConfig)

Owner: "do another mathematical analysis of all the different matchups that can happen for testing balance". Made with `tools/balance/matchups.py` (sections 1-3) and `tools/balance/matchups2.py` (4-6): sim105's fight model with the current rules (NpcHitsIgnoreHurtWindow true, so no hit turns or hurt-window losses on NPC targets; attacker cap 4; damage spread 0.2), the per-faction health, damage and prices of `ModConfig/lotrnpcs-factions.json`, 200 fights per pairing. Rider stats as 0.0.130 (warrior x (captain hire / warrior hire)^0.82); guard, shock and rider cost a warrior's hire plus the promotion (a rider: the captain's promotion). Calibrated against the game: 38 goblin v 26 Dunlending warriors leaves 19 goblins in the sim (game 12-17), 3 Rohan riders v 10 Dunlending warriors kill 4.6 in the sim (game, 0.0.132: 3 and 5).

Charge model for 0.0.133 (untested in game, assumptions): a rider's blow is a charge hit (x2) 70% of the time (the rest boxed in), and blows at a charging rider land half the time (it is out of reach while riding out).

## Headlines
1. Wild zone groups are even: every faction 0.48-0.52, one group or four a side (section 5). Groups scale with MaxFollowers, which is what keeps them level.
2. Full followings of the same rank (what a player leads) are mostly even for recruits, warriors and captains (0.40-0.60), with two exceptions growing with size: Misty Mountains goblins too strong (warriors 0.62 / 0.68 / 0.73 at x1 / x2 / x4) and dwarves, wicked dwarves, elves and tormented elves too weak (0.32-0.42 at x4).
3. Archers are the most lopsided rank: goblin archer followings 0.82-0.95, orc and Dunlending 0.61-0.74, elf and dwarf 0.20-0.32.
4. Riders by faction are far apart: Dunlending riders 0.95-0.99, Isengard 0.78-0.86, goblin 0.84-0.92, but Mordor, Angmar and Gundabad riders 0.06-0.14. The cause is 0.0.130's scale from each faction's captain/warrior price ratio (Dunlendings 35/12 = x2.41, orcs 29/12 = x2.03).
5. Equal coin is not balanced and size makes it worse: cheap many-unit factions win (orc, goblin and Dunlending warriors 0.74-0.91 at 360 coin; elf and dwarf warriors 0.19-0.22), and inside every faction recruits beat every other rank at equal coin (recruits 5 coin, warriors 15: three times the bodies). Without hit turns (since NpcHitsIgnoreHurtWindow) numbers count for more than in the earlier balance fits. Follower limits keep players from buying 48 recruits, so equal followings (2) matter more in play than equal coin.
6. Riders at their real price (warrior hire + captain promotion, 51 for Men) lose every equal-coin fight against their own warriors when standing (0.00-0.17); with the charge model they win 0.65-1.00. So the charge is the deciding factor, and its strength must be measured in game before anything is tuned.

## In-game tests that would confirm the sim (each side on one spot, 15-24 blocks apart, battle log on)
1. Wild-group check: 3 Gondor groups' worth (15 mixed: 5 recruits, 6 warriors, 3 archers, 1 captain) v the same for Mordor scaled by 6/5 (18). Expect about even.
2. Goblin strength: 32 goblin warriors v 20 Gondor warriors (full followings x4). Sim: goblins about 0.75.
3. Archers: 16 goblin archers v 8 elf archers (x2). Sim: goblins about 0.9; elves should lose clearly.
4. Dwarves: 16 dwarf warriors v 20 Gondor warriors (x4). Sim: dwarves about 0.35.
5. Riders by faction: 6 Dunlending riders v 6 Mordor riders. Sim: Dunlendings about 0.98.
6. Charge (0.0.133): 3 Rohan riders v 10 Dunlending warriors, twice; count charge hits and nocontact. Sim standing: riders lose (kill about 4.6); charge model: riders win. The real result sets the charge model.
7. Equal coin, recruits: 24 Gondor recruits (120 coin) v 8 Gondor warriors (120 coin). Sim: recruits win every time.

## Fix ideas (not built)
- Riders: one rider scale for every faction (e.g. 2.2) instead of the price ratio, then tune once 0.0.133's charge is measured.
- Archers: refit archer health/damage per faction so full followings sit near 0.5 (goblin archers down, elf and dwarf archers up).
- Goblins and dwarves/elves at large sizes: small health or damage trims, or count the size effect when fitting (fit at x2 instead of x1).
- If equal coin is the goal, prices need refitting under the no-hit-turn rules (recruits cost too little for what they do).
