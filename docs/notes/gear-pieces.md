# Gear pieces per NPC (2026-11-14, read only)

Owner, 2026-11-14: "Can the gear be simplified at all to not be so heavy on performance". Measured from the generated entity files (build/gen, 0.0.126) and the item and shape JSON in the installed mods (`%APPDATA%\VintagestoryData\Mods`) and the vanilla assets. Every element counted recursively. Made by `tools/research/gear_pieces.py <out.json>` then `tools/research/gear_pieces_report.py <out.json>`.

Why it matters: the client animates each visible NPC every frame (about 30 us per NPC in the 0.0.116 measurements, esr-afteranim), and that work scales with the elements of its composite shape. A tiny rivet costs the same as a breastplate. The body itself is only 20 elements.

Totals per NPC below are body (20) + the outfit's head, body and leg armor + the weapon (typical: the average of its weapon list; worst: the heaviest) + the shield (typical: half its average, as about half carry one; worst: the heaviest). 3 items didn't resolve (`game:armor-head-chain-iron@face`, `game:clothes-shoulder-apronbrown`, `game:clothes-shoulder-woolen-scarf`) and count 0.

## 3. Covered pieces

Every outfit is one helmet, one body armor and one leg armor (2 sets have none, 7 have two pieces, 1 hobbit set has four clothes). No set has two items in the same clothes category, and no item's `disableElements` hides another worn item's elements. So no whole piece can be dropped without a visible change. Not checked: elements inside one item that are buried inside its other elements (would need a geometric test).

## 4. Tiny decorations

Elements with any side under 0.5 voxel (rivets, trims, flat plates and edges): **1361 of the 1944 elements (70%) of the 15 heaviest items.** Per item they are in the "tiny" column of table 1. They still cost a full element each per frame.

## Options (nothing built)
1. Lighter variants per rank (our own choice, no other mod touched): give recruits and warriors the lighter sets and keep the 100-190 element pieces for captains, guards and riders only. Biggest single wins: Misty Mountains' Orc heavier body armor (192), the Mordor heavy servant armor (184), the evil dwarf heavier armor (140), Gundabad bone armor and leggings (131 + 114), the Arnor heavy helmet (113), our own kite shield (128).
2. Our own kite shield (`orcweapon-kiteshield`, 128 elements, 42 tiny) is the one heavy piece we own: rebuild it with fewer elements.
3. JSON patches that remove the tiny elements from other mods' armor shapes (like tools/patches/angmar-stepparent.json): up to about 70% fewer elements on the heaviest pieces, but it changes those mods' armor for players too, the detail is visible up close, and it needs redoing when they update. Owner's call.
4. Not possible cheaply: a lower-detail model at distance (VS builds one composite shape per entity; swapping it costs a full rebuild).
