# Balance check, 2026-11-14 (Claude, offline; no numbers changed)

Owner, 2026-11-14: "Work on balance again". Sim: `tools/balance/sim105.py` rules (hit turns, cap 4, +-20% spread, melee cycle 2.6-3.0 s, 90% land) on the shipped `FactionTiers` (src/Factions.cs; the owner's ModConfig has the same Misty Mountains 22/2.8 and Dunlending 28/3.6 warriors). Scripts: `tools/balance/matrix129.py`, `tools/balance/cap129.py`.

## The sim matches the game once you count who actually fought
In-game, same staging (38 goblin warriors v 26 Dunlending warriors): 0.0.122 Dunlendings 24-7, 0.0.123 26-10, 0.0.124 24-2. Only 24, 25 and 21 goblins ever swung. Measured in those logs: swings every 3.0-3.2 s, goblins landed 100%, Dunlendings 79-93% (the sim's 2.6-3.0 s and 90% are close).

| goblins v 26 Dunlendings (sim) | goblin wins | goblins left | Dunlendings left |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 24 | 0.00 | 0 | 26.0 |
| 30 | 0.00 | 0 | 21.1 |
| 35 | 0.62 | 5.1 | 3.4 |
| 38 | 1.00 | 17.0 | 0 |
| 42 | 1.00 | 27.0 | 0 |

So with all 38 fighting, the goblins should win clearly; with the 21-25 that fought, the sim gives the lopsided Dunlending wins seen in game. The losses are an engagement problem (stragglers that never join: 0.0.123/0.0.124/0.0.129 work), not the health and damage numbers. Parity is at about 35 goblins to 26 Dunlendings, i.e. the following sizes (8 goblins per 6 Dunlendings).

## Same-rank warriors, following sizes (Men 5, Uruk 5, Levy 6, Orc 6, Goblin 8, Elf 4, Dwarf 4): average win rate
x1: Men 0.47, Uruk 0.58, Levy 0.51, Orc 0.47, Goblin 0.51, Elf 0.47, Dwarf 0.44. x4 (20 men etc.): 0.45, 0.64, 0.52, 0.48, 0.55, 0.45, 0.41. Balanced as designed (Uruk a bit strong, more so in big battles; dwarves a bit weak). Single pairs range 0.23 to 0.79 at x4 (Goblin v Elf 0.79, Elf v Goblin 0.23).

## Knife edge
Fights are winner-takes-most: 30 v 26 loses everything and kills 5; 38 v 26 wins and loses 21. Equal coins by single warrior price (180 coins: Men 12, Levy 15, Goblin 20, Elf 9, Dwarf 9) look wildly unbalanced (Elf 0.11, Dwarf 0.09) only because 9 elves instead of the 10 that 2.5 followings would be is one unit short; one NPC flips the result. The attacker cap makes no difference in the sim (cap 2, 3, 4 and none give the same numbers): the hit turns (one hit on a target every 0.55-0.75 s) limit swarming first.

## Options offered to the owner
1. Keep the numbers; finish the engagement work (every NPC of an army in reach joins).
2. Small trims: Uruk-hai warriors a little weaker in big battles (0.64 at x4), dwarves a little stronger (0.41).
3. If closer, less all-or-nothing fights are wanted: that is a rule change (e.g. a wounded NPC backs off, or a longer hit-turn gap), not health and damage.
