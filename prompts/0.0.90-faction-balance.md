# 0.0.90 job script: factions "unequally equal"

Saved 2026-11-04 (second account, new chat), **revision 2** (owner: "create a prompt and follow it to revise this update and to improve in all aspects and details"; the prompt is `claude/prompts/0.0.90-revision-prompt.md`). **Built as 0.0.90 on 2026-11-05** (factions 489 and spawn zones pass; not tested in game; see HANDOFF section 3 for the two build-time calls: follow overflow on load guards instead of dismissing, borrowed guards go back before own followers are dismissed). Base: the 0.0.89 bundle, source hash `c60e5750797f9c36`. Every code fact below was read in that source this session. Every number comes from the simulator runs listed in section 4.

Owner's ask: "The next thing to balance is making the factions unequally equal. like orcs would have a lower hp pool with a little less damage but be cheaper to hire and be able to have more follow at once." Then: "think of balancing numbers yourself for all factions."

## 1. Decisions (owner's words, in the order asked)

1. **What comes out even: a full warband** (owner: "B"). A player's full following of any faction should win about as often as any other's. Price mainly sets how painful losses are to replace.
2. **Mixed armies with follow shares.** The owner first said "Players should only use the units in their faction", then changed their mind: "Follow shares". Owner's wording for the rule: *"Mixed armies: a player can hire from every faction they are allied to. Each follower uses 1/(its faction's follow limit) of the following, and the same kind of share applies to the total roster. Who attacks whom doesn't change."* Hostility stays as it is (owner: "A").
   - **"Allied to" (owner confirmed "A", 2026-11-04):** every faction whose NPCs count the player as a friend. That means the player's own factions (race, `/group`, or `/recruits faction set`), plus any faction allied to one of them **with `ShareFriends` on**. This is exactly today's hire check (`HiringSystem.StateFor`, "member" mode: `FactionSystem.IsFriend`, which walks `friendsFrom` = the faction and its allies that share friends). So it needs no code change, only clearer texts. Why not allies without `ShareFriends`: their aggressive NPCs attack the player on sight (`WantsToAttack` makes `StateFor` return null, so the talk menu never opens), and letting them hire would change who attacks whom. To let Mordor players hire Isengard units, an admin sets that alliance to `ally` with `ShareFriends` true. No factions are allied by default (config v16).
3. **Existing worlds over the new limits: dismiss** (owner: "B", then "BB" = for both). Recruits over the total roster go first, then followers over the follow share, most recently hired first each time. In normal play nothing is dismissed (section 3.4).
4. **Wild groups scale with the follow limit** (owner: "A"). Group sizes and captain escorts scale, and the pause limit counts shares.
5. **The numbers are config settings per faction** (owner: "A"): faction config v19.
6. **Groupings** as proposed, except the owner's "Uruk are the elite units compared to dunlending". Uruks get Men's follow limit (owner: "A") "but they should have slightly more health and damage then men", accepted as the strongest warband, balanced by cost (owner: "A"). **Revision 2 finding:** the better simulator puts that edge at about 55% against Men, not the "about 3 in 4" told to the owner. Owner chose (a), 2026-11-04: keep the stats, soften the cost to x1.1 (prices 6/17/44/17, promotion 11/11/33, roster 15).

## 3. Rules, exactly

### 3.1 Shares
- `FollowShare(f) = 1 / MaxFollowers(f)`, `RosterShare(f) = 1 / MaxRecruits(f)`.
- **Follow used** = the sum of FollowShare over the recruits the player leads that are told to follow: `HiringSystem.Followers`, which counts their own plus shared guards they borrowed (`Led`). **Roster used** = the sum of RosterShare over `HiringOwner.Recruits` (their own only, loaded or not).
- One more of faction f fits if `used + share(f) <= 1 + 1e-6`. Exact, never over 1 (owner chose "a", 2026-11-04, over a 1.05 tolerance or "the last one always fits"). Leftover share is the price of mixing; the room line shows what still fits.
- Each recruit's faction is read from its type (`FactionSystem.FactionOf`). For unloaded recruits it comes from the new saved map `HiringOwner.RecruitFactions` (entity id to faction). Recruits from saves before 0.0.90 aren't in the map until they next load. Until then each counts at the baseline roster share (1/global `MaxRecruits`, the Men share) and is never dismissed (my call: dismiss only what is known).
- A faction a config doesn't know (removed by an admin) uses the global baseline values.

### 3.2 Hiring (`HiringSystem.Hire`, `StateFor`)
- Who may hire which NPC is unchanged: "member" mode, `IsFriend` (decision 2).
- The roster check replaces `Hired >= Cap` with "does one more of this faction fit the roster".
- Past the follow share, a new recruit guards where it was hired, as now. The check becomes "does it fit the follow share" instead of `Followers.Count >= MaxFollowers`.
- Promotion keeps the faction (it swaps the type within a race), so shares don't change. `HiringSystem.Replace` copies the map entry to the new entity id.

### 3.3 Following (`Act` follow and borrow, orders menu `Follow` in Hiring.cs)
- Talk menu Follow and Borrow: allowed if the NPC's faction fits the follow share.
- Orders menu Follow: those already following stay. The others are tried nearest first, and each joins if it fits, so a goblin can still join after an elf didn't. The reply counts who stayed behind.
- A borrowed shared guard counts on the borrower's follow share by its own faction. It never counts on the borrower's roster.

### 3.4 Over the limits (decision 3)
- **When it runs:** when a player joins (`OnPlayerNowPlaying`), after `/recruits faction reload` (for online players), and when a recruit's faction is first learned on load (old saves, see 3.1). Not on normal play.
- **Order:**
  1. While roster used > 1, dismiss the most recently hired known recruit (the last in `HiringOwner.Recruits`; the list is in hire order, and promotion keeps the place). Loaded ones go now, as Dismiss in the talk menu. Unloaded ones go to `PendingDismiss` and are freed when they load, as `/recruits dismissall` does.
  2. Then, while follow used > 1, dismiss the most recently hired of the player's own followers.
  3. A borrowed guard over the share is given back instead: it guards where it stands, the same as when the borrower logs off. It is never dismissed, since it isn't the borrower's (my call).
- **Chat** (the player, if online) and **server log**: who left and why (texts in 3.7).

### 3.5 Health and damage at runtime
- **Health:** `EntityBehaviorFactionMember.Initialize` already calls `SyncMaxHealth(entity, TypeMaxHealth(...))`, which brings an NPC to its type's health and keeps its wounds (0.0.43). Pass the scaled health instead. A new NPC spawns at full scaled health; an existing one gets it on load and keeps its wounds. After `/recruits faction reload`, every loaded NPC of this mod is synced again.
- **Damage:** read the multiplier when the attack happens, so a reload applies at once. `AiTaskChestMeleeAttack` keeps the config's base damage and sets `damage = baseDamage x DamageMultiplier(faction)` in `StartExecute` (one dictionary lookup per swing). `AiTaskFactionShoot` (Archery.cs) sets `projectile.Damage = damage x DamageMultiplier(faction)` when shooting. Wild NPCs and recruits use the same values.

### 3.6 Wild groups (`SpawnZones.cs`, decision 4)
- `scale(f) = MaxFollowers(f) / global MaxFollowers` (the baseline is 5, so Orc 1.4, Elf 0.8).
- `GroupRanks` takes the scale: `GroupSizeAvg x s`, `GroupSizeVar x s`, `GroupSizeMax` = round(5 x s), at least 1. Captain escort maximum = round(`CaptainEscortMax` x s), at least `CaptainEscortMin`. So: Men 1 to 5 (escort 1 to 3), Levy 1 to 6 (1 to 4), Orc 1 to 7 (1 to 4), Goblin 1 to 8 (1 to 5), Elf and Dwarf 1 to 4 (1 to 2).
- **MaxNearby** counts weights: each zone NPC weighs `1 / scale` of its faction (a goblin 0.625, an elf 1.25). The spawn check and the rival check (`nearby.Count + ranks.Count < MaxNearby`) use the weights.
- The admin's `/recruits zone` test spawn uses the same `GroupRanks`.

### 3.7 Texts (plain English, no em dashes, "recruit" never "hireling")
Server-built and localized for the player (`Lang.GetL(player.LanguageCode, ...)`). The talk dialog's `Format` (`GuiDialogTalk`, Hiring.cs) passes `{0}` price, `{1}` coin, `{2}` cap, `{3}` owner, `{4}` hired, `{5}` follow cap. This round adds `{6}` = room text (`TalkState.Room`, a string), and stops using `{2}`, `{4}` and `{5}` in these keys:
- `talk-full`: "You lead as many recruits as anyone can keep in line. {6}"
- `talk-followfull`: "Your band is full. {6} Send someone off first."
- `talk-hired-guard`: "Your coin is good. Your band is full, so I'll keep watch here until you call for me."
- `cmd-follow-full`: "Recruits following you: {0}. {1} stayed behind because your band is full. {2}" ({2} = room text)
- **Room text** (new `room-line`): "Room in your band: {0}." The list holds the factions of the player's current followers plus the faction being hired or ordered, at most four, each as "{folk} {n}": "Room in your band: Mordor orcs 1, Men of Gondor 0." New `folk-<faction>` keys for all 18 factions: Men of Gondor, Riders of Rohan, Men of Dale, Dunedain of Arnor, Haradrim, Easterlings, Black Numenoreans, Dunlendings, Uruk-hai, Mordor orcs, Angmar orcs, Gundabad orcs, Goblins of the Misty Mountains, Elves, Tormented elves, Dwarves, Wicked dwarves, Hobbits.
- **Over-limit chat** (new `limits-dismissed`): "Your recruits were over the new limits, so {0} left your service: {1}." Plus `limits-dismissed-later` when some were away: "{0} more will leave when you next come near them."
- **#Instructions** (faction config), new lines: "MaxFollowers and MaxRecruits (per faction): how many of this faction's recruits can follow a player at once, and how many a player can have. Each recruit uses a share: 1/MaxFollowers of the following and 1/MaxRecruits of the roster, and the shares of all a player's recruits must add up to no more than 1, so factions can be mixed. The MaxFollowers and MaxRecruits at the top of the file are the baseline (the Men's) and the values for a faction without its own." / "HealthMultiplier and DamageMultiplier (per faction): the faction's NPCs have their type's health and damage times these (1 = unchanged). Damage tiers don't change." / "Wild groups: GroupSizeAvg, GroupSizeVar, GroupSizeMax and CaptainEscortMax in the spawning file are for a faction with the baseline MaxFollowers, and scale with each faction's MaxFollowers." The ShareFriends line gets "... and lets them hire each other's NPCs."
- Spawning #Instructions: the group size lines and MaxNearby ("counts NPCs by their faction's share: an orc counts 5/7 of a man").
- `docs/config-guide.md`: the same, plus the tier table of section 2.

## 4. How the numbers were found (and how far to trust them)

**Simulator:** `sim2.py` plus `analyse.py` (event driven; goes into `lotrnpcs/tools/balance/` at build time). It models:
- rank health and damage with the rounding rules above
- melee every 1.95 to 2.35 s with 90% landing (my guess)
- arrows every 3.1 to 3.9 s (1.1 s draw + 2 to 2.8 s) with 70% landing (my guess), shooting while melee walks in for 3 to 5 s
- at most 5 attackers per target (inner ring), each taking an enemy with the fewest attackers first
- a 0.8 to 1.5 s walk after a kill
- ranks drawn at random from a makeup, so every faction gets the same proportions on average. Rounding a small band's ranks had given 8 goblins two captains and 5 men one, which alone flipped results.

Makeups: mixed 40/40/20, all recruits, veterans 60/40 warrior/captain, and with 25% archers. Wild groups are made as `GroupRanks` makes them, scaled. Health multipliers were tuned until each tier's average over all makeups and wild groups was even (Uruk left out of the tuning). Tuning ran at 800 to 1500 fights per pairing and the final check at 2000.

**Final check, at 2000 fights per pairing:**

| Situation | Range of results |
|---|---|
| Mixed armies | 0.43 to 0.57 for every pair, Uruk included |
| Veterans | 0.31 to 0.66 |
| With archers | 0.43 to 0.58 |
| Wild group against wild group | 0.44 to 0.56 |
| Full-share mixed armies against a Men band | 0.48 to 0.52 (2 Elf + 2 Dwarf, 2 Dwarf + 4 Goblin, 1 Elf + 6 Goblin, 2 Uruk + 3 Men) |
| Mixes that leave share unused, against a Men band | 0.28 to 0.41 (3 Men + 2 Orc at 0.89; 2 Elf + 3 Orc, 3 Orc + 4 Goblin and 3 Uruk + 2 Levy at 0.93) |
| Uruk against Men | 0.55 averaged over makeups (mixed 0.56, veterans 0.64, archers 0.55, recruits 0.50, wild 0.52) |
| Threat to a lone player | Men 1.00, Uruk 1.08, Levy 1.01, Orc 1.05, Goblin 1.00, Elf 1.03, Dwarf 1.02 |

The threat to a lone player is the damage they take killing a whole band (d x h x n(n+1)/2). So with groups scaled, a goblin group is as dangerous to a player as a group of men, even though each goblin dies faster. This replaces the first draft's worry that goblins would be easy for solo players.

**Limits:**
- **Fights are almost decided before they start.** The same makeup gives nearly the same result each time, and whole hits decide them. An army of one rank sits on one hit threshold: all-recruit armies range from 0.04 to 0.95. Orc recruits need 10 hits on a 23-health Uruk recruit but 9 on a 22-health man, so the 5% Uruk edge acts like 11%. Mixed ranks average this out.
- **Goblins sit on a step.** Goblin recruits at 12 health die to 4 Men hits, at 13 to 5. That alone moves Goblins from 0.40 to 0.54 on average (with damage 0.75). So health 0.57 puts the recruit on 13, and damage 0.73 brings the average back to even.
- **Left out:** terrain, pathing, knockback, formations, battle lines (off by default since 0.0.85), the edge guard, players' own skill, and Combat Overhaul's effect between NPCs (none: NPCs have no armor resist). The 90% and 70% hit rates are guesses.
- **In-game testing decides.** Because the numbers are config settings, the owner can retune without a build.

## 9. Risks

- The hit rates and walk times in the simulator are guesses. Real fights have more chance in them, which pulls results toward 0.5.
- Old saves: until each old recruit loads once, the roster counts it at the Men share. The first join may therefore dismiss fewer than needed, and more go as recruits load (by design, converging).
- Dismissal is irreversible. Players lose recruits they paid for (owner's choice). Log every one.
- Prices only change where they were the shipped defaults. An admin who customised prices keeps them and should check them against the shares.
