# 0.0.91 job script: per-rank balance, hit spread, lifetime despawn, roster line

Built 2026-11-05 (second account, same chat as 0.0.90; base the 0.0.89 bundle, changes in `source/source.patch`). Factions 493 and spawn zones pass, DLL 0 warnings. **Not tested in game** (checklist section 91).

## What the owner said, in order

1. After testing 0.0.90: "in the 5 v 7 of rohan (men) vs orcs (mordor) the men won losing only 1 guy", then "it was a 5 v 7 of warrior ranks on both sides".
   - Cause: whole hits. A Rohan warrior (4 damage) kills an orc warrior (22 health) in 6 hits; an orc warrior (3.4) needs 10 on a Rohan warrior (34). The simulator gave Men 73% in warrior against warrior.
   - 0.0.90 was balanced on mixed ranks. Every NPC of a rank has the same health and damage, so same-rank fights land at about 30% or 70%, never in between.
2. On how to fix it (A spread, B per-rank numbers, C leave it): "B, or change the amount of recruits you have if at max should still be a 50/50 chance one side defeats the other for same rank fights".
   - Follow limits are per faction, not per rank, so changing them can't fix one rank alone, and one more unit is a big step. Per-rank numbers chosen.
3. Mid-fit: "balance it for all factions on an average, not just men".
4. Shown that per-rank numbers alone leave single pairs lopsided (warriors: Men beat elves 91% but lose to goblins 85%): "add a ±20% spread on hits. for right now, even on average is enough".
5. "I also just considered, have recruits that arent hired, despawn over time so areas dont get utterly flooded". Options were A (lifetime limit), B (tighter existing timers) and C (per-zone cap). Owner: "A".
6. "Also I think that since the number has changed, players should be notified how many recruits they have left in their capacity".

## What was built

- **Per-rank health and damage** (`FactionTiers` in Factions.cs; per faction `Health` and `Damage` tables by rank, replacing 0.0.90's `HealthMultiplier`/`DamageMultiplier`).
  - Fitted with `tools/balance/fitspread.py`: joint hill climbing over all seven tiers per rank, common random numbers, Men free but pulled to base, a small pull toward each tier's 0.0.90 flavour, and the spread in the fights. The result is in `tools/balance/ranks-0.0.91.json`.
  - Fresh-fight check: averages 0.45 to 0.57 (Uruks 0.53 to 0.57), every single pair 0.37 to 0.63, Rohan against Mordor warriors 0.52.
  - Tables (health / damage, recruit, warrior, captain, archer):
    - Men 22/34/48/34, 3.05/4/7.05/3
    - Uruk 23/32/48/35, 2.95/4.3/7.15/2.95
    - Levy 18/29/41/28, 2.7/3.4/6/2.6
    - Orc 15/27/31/22, 2.5/2.8/5.95/2.45
    - Goblin 16/21/31/19, 1.85/2.85/4.75/2.2
    - Elf 27/37/53/37, 3.65/5.4/9.6/4.25
    - Dwarf 30/46/63/46, 3.25/4.35/7.9/3.4
  - Health is applied through `SyncMaxHealth` (`ScaledMaxHealth` picks the faction's value for `EntityBehaviorFactionMember.RankOf`; rankless NPCs keep their type's). Damage is read at each swing and each arrow. Damage tiers are unchanged.
- **Hit spread:** top-level `DamageSpread` 0.2, clamped 0 to 0.9 (`FactionConfig.Spread`). `FactionTiers.Spread(damage, spread, roll)` is applied in `AiTaskChestMeleeAttack.StartExecute` and `AiTaskFactionShoot` (projectile damage). It covers wild NPCs and recruits, and players' incoming hits too (same average).
- **Config v20:**
  - A version 19 file's multipliers become per-rank tables. Multipliers still as shipped get the fitted table (`Health090`/`Damage090` in each tier). Changed ones become base x the admin's multiplier (health rounded).
  - The old fields are read once (`OldHealthMultiplier`, `OldDamageMultiplier`, `ShouldSerialize` false).
  - Rank names compare in lower case, missing ranks fill from the tier, health is clamped to whole numbers 1 to 10000 and damage to 0 to 1000.
  - `DescribeFactions` shows "health 15/27/31/22, damage 2.5/2.8/5.95/2.45" and "Hits vary by 20% either way."
- **Lifetime despawn** (SpawnZones.cs):
  - `MaxLifetimeMinutes` 20 and `LifetimeDespawnDistance` 64 in the spawning file. A zone NPC's age goes in `lotrnpcsZoneAgeSeconds`.
  - Past the limit, the NPC despawns (Expire) when no player is within 64 blocks and it isn't fighting (`EntityBehaviorFactionMember.Fighting`). 0 turns it off.
  - Hired recruits (origin "hired") and creative-spawned NPCs are never affected.
- **Roster line:**
  - `TalkState.RosterRoom` (`HiringSystem.RosterRoom`: the NPC's faction first, then the factions of known recruits, at most four) is talk placeholder `{7}`, lang `roster-line` "Room on your roster: {0}.".
  - The talk menu shows the band and roster lines in grey under the greeting of a hireable NPC and after a hire (`GuiDialogTalk.Status`).
  - Chat on every hire: `hired-room` "You hired {0}. {1} {2}".
  - `talk-full` now shows the roster line.

## Calls I made (tell the owner)

- Men's numbers moved slightly with the fit (recruit damage 3.05, captain 7.05) so their own average comes out even too.
- The roster line lists at most four factions.
- Fighting NPCs never despawn by age. The 64 blocks is meant as "out of sight", but players with a long view distance may still see one vanish at 65 blocks.

## Risks

- The simulator's hit rates and walk times are guesses. Real fights have more chance in them (movement, knockback), which pulls toward even.
- Single pairs are within about 37% to 63% only with the spread. With `DamageSpread` 0 they go back to step results.
- The roster line counts pre-0.0.90 recruits at the Men's share until they load.
