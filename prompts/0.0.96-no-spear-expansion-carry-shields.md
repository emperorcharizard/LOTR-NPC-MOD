# 0.0.96: no Spear Expansion, Gundabad spear, shields closer, bigger captain's sword, shoulder carry, Gundabad captain and ears

Owner, 2026-11-07 (second account, new chat on the 0.0.95 base), in four messages during the round. All built.

## Asks, in the owner's words, and what was built

1. "Let the gundabad orcs use the moria spear."
   - gen `WEAPONS_OF["gundabadorc"]` = orc dagger, orc axe, Moria spear (`mmspear`, held and thrust as the Misty
     Mountains orcs do; Gundabad have no shield). All three ranks.
2. "remove the not only spears expansion dependancy, as it wont be implemented into the server"
   - gen `PLACEHOLDERS` (the eight `spearexpantion:` items: pike, halberd, war pick, flanged and common mace, khopesh,
     bartka, seax) deleted, with their animations. `WEAPON_PREFS` keeps only factions with more than the fallback:
     Gondor captains the captain's sword; Haradrim the Haradrim spear only (no falx, owner 0.0.86); elves the elven
     spear or the falx; dwarves and wicked dwarves unchanged. Everyone else falls back to `WEAPONS_OF`: the game's iron
     falx for Gondor recruits and warriors, Rohan, Dale, Arnor, Dunlendings, Black Numenoreans, tormented elves,
     Uruks, Easterlings. NPCs holding a Spear Expansion item pick again on load (`PickWeapon`: not in the list). No C#.
3. "make a list of the factions and weapons they need to fill out their kits"
   - `docs/model-checklist.md` rewritten (project `claude/model-checklist.md`): per faction, what it carries now and
     the weapons still needed, the owner's 0.0.82 preferences marked, plus shields and bows. Recommendation in it: the
     falx-only factions first, a shared Mannish spear and sword first.
4. "move the dwarven shields a little bit closer to their models, same with the elven shields, and the gondor shields"
   (screenshot: dwarf recruit, shield standing off the arm).
   - Measured on the race's own body (new scratch tools, method as `tools/preview/shieldfit.py`): on each shield a
     small back part (tower shield's lower strap, iron shield's posts, curved shield's rim, round shield's handle,
     elven shield's bar posts) touched the 0.5 px sleeve margin first, leaving the plate about 1 px off. New
     `SHIELD_IN` (model px): tower, iron, elven, round 1.0; curved Gondor 0.75. Elven and round plates now sit on the
     sleeve; the dwarf and curved Gondor back parts sink about 0.6 px into the arm, behind the plate.
5. "the gondorian anduril sword is also very small" (screenshot). Asked: 1.5x, 1.25x or full scale; owner chose
   **1.5x**: scale 0.459, 2.0 blocks (was 0.306, 1.35 blocks, the owner's earlier "53 inches"). Left hand still on the
   handle.
6. "the gundabad captain should not spawn in the bone armor like it does": the bone set is gone from captains (warlord
   body and legs with the warlord, tusked or the owner's helmet only). Captains already spawned keep their gear.
7. "with the new gundabad helmet, the ears stick out from the sides": `lotrnpcs:helmethidehair` takes
   `hideEarsUnder` (wildcards; gen `EAR_HIDING_HELMETS` = the owner's Gundabad Helmet only). Ear variants name their
   own shape, so `HairShapePath` also reads a variant's `shape.base`. C# change (HelmetHair.cs).
8. "Fix the way the poleaxes are held, this great hammer isnt held correctly" (screenshot: dwarf captain, warhammer
   standing 1.5 blocks over its head). Asked (render `previews/hold-before-0.0.96.png`): hands apart, on the
   shoulder, hammer grip higher, head down; owner chose **on the shoulder**.
   - `tools/anims/poleaxehold.json` is now a shoulder carry: right arm and ItemAnchor only (elbow down by the ribs,
     hand in front of the shoulder, shaft on the right shoulder top rising 28 degrees to the back, head behind).
     Solved with scipy against the human and dwarf bodies together: shaft within 0.4 px of the shoulder top on both,
     nothing through head or torso. Poleaxe, battleaxe and warhammer hold only the right arm and item (`CARRY`), so the
     left arm walks. The chop is unchanged and starts from the carry.
   - The captain's sword keeps the 0.0.76 guard, renamed `greatswordhold` (new file, same keyframes; my call: the owner
     flagged only the poleaxe-type weapons).
   - Previews `previews/shoulder-carry-dwarf-0.0.96.png`, `previews/shoulder-carry-human-0.0.96.png`.
