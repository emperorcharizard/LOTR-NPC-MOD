# 0.0.97: the owner's Haradrim sword, curved sword, light and heavy shields

Owner, 2026-11-07 (second account, same chat as 0.0.96): four Blockbench files sent without notes:
`haradrim_sword.bbmodel`, `haradrim_curved_sword.bbmodel`, `haradrim_light_shield.bbmodel`,
`haradrim_heavy_shield.bbmodel`. Kept in `tools/weapons/source/`, converted at build time (`source`). Built with my
defaults, all told to the owner.

## What was built

- **Weapons** (gen `HARADRIM_WEAPONS`): `orcweapon-hdsword` "Haradrim sword" (grip (3, 0.75, 8), 41 units, 1.54
  blocks) and `orcweapon-hdcurvedsword` "Haradrim curved sword" (grip (1.85, 0.75, 8), 34 units, 1.28 blocks), normal
  weapon scale, rotation x 180 (edge on the convex +Y side, turned like the Mordor scimitar's; the curved sword has
  no marked edge: my reading), one-handed, falx slash. Haradrim recruits, warriors and captains: spear, sword, curved
  sword, weight 1 each (no falx, owner 0.0.86).
- **Shields** (gen `HD_LIGHT_SHIELD`, `HD_HEAVY_SHIELD`): both face +Z with grip and forearm strap behind. Orientation
  chosen by testing the four quarter turns of the Mordor hold: the one where the forearm runs from the grip toward
  the strap (light: Mordor rotation; heavy: +90 about Z). Grips at the bar middles, light (10, 4.75, 5.9), heavy
  (16, 0.75, 5.55); moved in 0.6 / 0.5 px so the backs rest on the sleeve (0.0.96's rule). Lowest point while
  walking (human body): heavy 1.1 px, light 3.5 px above the ground.
- **Shields by rank** (my call): `SHIELD_OF` values may now be a dict by rank. Haradrim recruits the light shield,
  warriors and captains the heavy shield (as their armor goes: infantry, heavy infantry, warlord). `ShieldChance`
  unchanged; archers none.
- Preview `previews/haradrim-weapons-shields-0.0.97.png` (beside the Mordor scimitar).
