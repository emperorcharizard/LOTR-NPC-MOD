## 4. Melee targets need line of sight

- Owner: "npcs seem to be trying to target npcs through walls, can that be changed to be enemies like, not behind walls?" Chose **A**.
- A: picking a new target (`AiTaskFactionSeek.IsTargetableEntity`) needs a clear line from the NPC's eyes to the target (blocks only, like archers' `ClearShot`). Once fighting, a target out of sight is kept for about 5 s (follows round corners and through doors), then dropped. Striking back at an attacker always allowed.
- My calls, told to the owner: applies to players as well as NPCs (owner didn't object); glass and leaves block sight unless treated as see-through (decide when building: probably use the game's collision boxes, so glass blocks, leaves block).
- Cost: one ray per candidate per scan (scan every 1 s); cache per candidate for the scan.

## 5. Archers on a cliff edge (owner: "NPC archers have a hard time shooting at someone below them"; "not try at all on a cliffs edge")

- Cause (my reading, matches "not try at all"): `AiTaskFactionShoot.ClearShot` traces from the archer's eye to the target's chest; looking steeply down from a few tenths of a block back from the edge, that line clips the lip block by its feet, so no target is ever picked. The 0.0.88 edge guard keeps NPCs about 0.6 blocks short of a drop over 4 blocks, so they can't step to the lip.
- Fix: trace from the arrow's real start (eye, 0.4 ahead toward the target, as `Shoot` spawns it) and let the archer step up to the lip (edge guard: standing at the edge is fine, only a step over the drop is stopped) when a target below is hidden by the edge. Check the arc doesn't clip the lip either (`SolveArc` low arc starts downward). Test: archer on a 10-block cliff, target 5 to 15 out.
