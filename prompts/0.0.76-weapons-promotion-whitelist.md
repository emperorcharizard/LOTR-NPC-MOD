## 1. Blockbench converter (new tool)

**[owner]** "read block bench files and their textures and put that in vintage story as a single file and single
texture".

New `tools/bbmodel_to_vs.py <in.bbmodel> <out.json> <out.png>`:
- Reads Blockbench 5 projects in Vintage Story format (`meta.model_format == "formatVS"`).
- Keeps the group tree: each group becomes a parent element with its `rotationOrigin` and rotations; cubes become
  children. Element rotations, face UVs and face UV rotations are kept.
- Drops zero-size cubes (the `*_geo` placeholders) **[fact: elven spear 3, kite shield 1]**.
- Textures: embedded PNGs (`data:image/png;base64`) are decoded. One texture is written as is. Several are packed onto
  one sheet at a common pixels-per-UV-unit, and every face's UV is rewritten to its place on the sheet. A face whose UV
  runs past its texture's edge is an error (would need tiling), reported by name.
- Output is the model JSON (`textures: {atlas}`, `textureWidth/Height` = the UV size of the sheet) plus the PNG, ready
  for gen's `atlas` entries. The converted files go in `tools/weapons/` and `tools/weapons/atlas/`; the `.bbmodel`
  stays in `source/` as the original.
- Checked by rendering each converted model next to a render made straight from the `.bbmodel` (same geometry, same
  pixels on each face).

## 7. Recruits

### 7.1 Promotion **[owner, all of it unless marked]**
- **XP:** a hired NPC of the recruit or warrior rank that hits an NPC from this mod gets 1 XP if that NPC dies within
  10 seconds of the hit. Several hitters each get the point. Monsters, animals and players give nothing. Archers and
  captains earn nothing.
- **Cap:** XP stops at the requirement and resets to 0 on promotion.
- **Recruit, 15 XP:** promote to warrior or to archer for 10 coins. **Warrior, 40 XP:** promote to captain for 30 coins.
  Archers have no second rank. Hobbits have no ranks.
- **Where:** a line in the right-click talk menu ("Promote to warrior (10 coins)" etc.), shown when the XP is full.
  Paid in the faction's hire coin from hotbar and backpack, like hiring. Prices in the faction config next to
  `HirePrices` as `PromotePrices` **[default]** (config version 16).
- **Display:** a line `XP: (x/x)` under the name and health, seen by the owner and the players in the owner's /group.
- **On promotion:** owner's choices kept (name, face, weapon if the new rank can carry it, orders, stance, guard post,
  shared-guard state, borrower). Armor becomes the new rank's set. Helmet: if it wore one, the new rank's helmet; if the
  owner took it off, it stays off. Health, damage and title change to the new rank's.
- **How [default]:** promotion keeps the same NPC (same entity id, so the recruit list and save data don't move) and
  switches what makes the rank: the rank attribute, max health, melee damage, gear set, helmets list, title. Recruit to
  archer also needs the archer's shooting AI and bow; if that can't be switched in place safely, archer promotion
  replaces the NPC with an archer carrying everything above over, and the recruit list is updated in the same step.
  Decided during the build, reported to the owner.
- New lang lines: promote menu lines, "not enough coins", "promoted" chat line, `XP: ({0}/{1})`.

## 11. As built (2026-11-04, 0.0.76)

The owner said "go ahead and act" with the defaults in section 9 except item 7. Decided or found during the build:

- **Falx and longbow codes** come from the wiki's image names (`game:blade-falx-iron`, `game:bow-long`): the PC link
  was down, so the game files couldn't be read. A wrong code only leaves the hand empty with a client-log warning. The
  game's own items carry the game's own hand transforms (the NPC body is the player body), so no hold was solved.
- **Promotion swaps the NPC** for one of the new rank (section 7.1's fallback, used for every promotion): the game
  rebuilds health, AI, gear and the helmet list from the type on every load, so an in-place change would undo itself.
- **Poleaxe hold**: the right hand moves 0.25 blocks up the shaft (new `poleaxehold`), and the poleaxe's grip moves the
  same distance so the weapon itself stays put; the left hand is now below the right.
- **Shields** needed a 1.4 px slide out along the face to clear the forearm, like the Mordor shield's 1 px.
- **Return to post** only needed adding for guards on Loose formation; holders and formation guards already returned.
- **XP line** is added by a Harmony postfix on `EntityAgent.GetInfoText`, the only way to place it after the game's own
  lines.
- **Race textures**: 1448 KB to about 1000 KB, pixels identical.
