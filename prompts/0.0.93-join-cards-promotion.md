## Built

### Join menu (`src/Joining.cs`)

- Three columns of cards (232 x 60) under section headings Free Peoples (gold), Shadow (ember), Others (grey), each heading with a thin rule.
- Sections from the design doc's blocs (`JoinSystem.BlocOf`, sent as `JoinOption.Bloc`). **My calls (shown in the mockup, not objected to):** Gundabad (unset in the design doc) under Shadow; Dunlendings (Independent), Wicked dwarves and Tormented elves (unset) under Others; factions an admin adds under Others. Layout only: allies nobody.
- Card (`GuiElementFactionCard`, a custom GuiElement): dark panel, the faction's portrait on the left (the creative-tab spawn item of its warrior, else recruit, captain, archer, else any of its types: `JoinSystem.PortraitType`, sent as `JoinOption.Portrait`; drawn by the game's `GuiElementPassiveItemSlot`, which the portrait framing was made for), the name in bold (wraps to two lines), "N followers · M total" (owner's words), a green tag only for a stance other than aggressive (Hobbits: defensive). Hover highlight; click = press and release on the same card (so the confirm screen's buttons under the cursor can't fire on the same click).
- Confirm screen shows the chosen card beside "Join the ...? This is permanent".
- Legend under the cards: "Followers: how many recruits can walk with you at once. Total: how many recruits you can have in all." Intro, legend, confirm and notice texts size to their wrapped height (`TextHeight`).
- Lang: `join-row` removed; new `join-followers`, `join-follower`, `join-total`, `join-legend`, `join-bloc-free/shadow/other`; `join-explain` reworded ("Pick the people you fight for...").
- Offline layout preview `tools/preview/joinmenu.py` -> `previews/join-menu-0.0.93.png` (DejaVu Sans, wider than the game's font, so wrapping there is the worst case; faces are placeholders).

### Promotion prices (`src/Factions.cs`)

- `FactionTiers.PromotePrice(rank, hire)` = hire price of the new rank less 3, less 4 for a captain, never below 0; `PromoteFor(hirePrices)`. Tier `Promote` tables are derived from the tier's hire prices; the old ones kept as `Promote090`.
- New defaults (warrior/archer/captain): Men 12/12/36, Uruk 14/14/40, Levy 10/10/29, Orc 8/8/25, Goblin 6/6/21, Elf and Dwarf 16/16/46.
- Ranks a file leaves out get the faction's own hire price less the discount (so an admin's hire prices count).
- **Config v22:** promotion prices still as 0.0.90 to 0.0.92 shipped them become the faction's hire prices less the discount; prices the admin changed stay. The v19 step now also derives from the hire prices.
- XP rules unchanged. #Instructions, config guide, the owner's PDF updated.

## Risks (untested in game)

- The card is the mod's first custom GUI element: text placement under the game's font and GUI scales, the hover texture, and the portrait inside a dialog (it was only ever drawn in the creative inventory).
- Mouse handling relies on `GuiElement.OnMouseDownOnElement` / `OnMouseUp` being called for interactive elements, as the game's buttons use them.
- Promoting now costs more in total than hiring the rank outright (Men recruit 5 + 12 = 17 vs a warrior at 15), on top of the XP. Told the owner before they chose.
