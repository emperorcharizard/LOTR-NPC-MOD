## 6. Combat questions (owner: "grill me for questions")

Asked one at a time (2026-11-04). Answers:

1. Biggest problem: **movement in a fight**.
2. Seen: **jitter or dance**, and **bunching up / clipping**.
3. Where: **NPC vs NPC battles** and **the owner's recruits fighting**.
4. The jitter: **turning in place** (facing snaps back and forth) and **in and out** (steps toward the enemy, backs
   off, steps in again, often without swinging).
5. The bunching: **one whole blob**, both sides merged in the middle.
6. Wanted instead: paired duels, battle lines, and today's rules fixed (all three).
7. Combined as: **lines that pair off**: a group forms a front; each front NPC duels the enemy opposite it; rear ranks
   step in when a gap opens; the jitter and overlap fixed underneath.
8. Outnumbered: **wrap the flanks**: extras join the end enemies 2 on 1, never more than 2 on one enemy NPC.
9. Who forms lines: **everyone but Loose**: wild groups and recruits in any formation; Loose followers and lone NPCs
   just duel.
10. Advance: **by stance**: wild NPCs and Aggressive/Raid recruits advance; Defensive recruits hold and let the
    enemy come within 6 blocks.
11. Also (owner's words): "the spacing between npcs might be too far, as they seem to always stay at that range when
    attacking, there should be a minimum, and a maximum range".
12. Duel range: **1.0 to 1.6 blocks** from the enemy's center: close in past 1.6, step back inside 1.0, never overlap.
13. Gap between neighbours in a line: **1.5 blocks** (config `CombatSpacing`, default 2.0 now; config version bump with
    the usual "only if unchanged" migration).

### Design notes for the build (mine, to check against the code first)

- Likely causes to confirm in `Battle.cs` / `Factions.cs` / `Hiring.cs` before changing anything:
  - "In and out": the surround spot sits at up to `MaxSurroundRadius` 2.2 while melee wants to be in reach, and the
    push-apart (`PushSpeed` 1.2, `CombatSpacing` 2.0) shoves attackers out of their spot; seek and melee then walk back
    in. Fix: one owner of movement per NPC per tick (duel spot), push only between same-side neighbours, and a dead
    band (1.0 to 1.6) where the NPC stands still and swings.
  - "Turning in place": the NPC faces its path target (the surround spot) while walking and its enemy while swinging,
    and the spot moves with the enemy. Fix: always face the duel partner while within 3 blocks of it; turn rate limit.
  - "Blob": vanilla entities don't collide, the surround spots of both sides land on each other, and wild groups seek
    the nearest enemy. Lines give each side its own ground.
- Lines: when a group (wild group, or a leader's formation followers) first sees an enemy group, take the line through
  its members' centre at right angles to the enemy group's centre; front rank slots 1.5 apart, as many as the enemy has
  front NPCs (+ flank slots for extras, rule 8); others stand 1.5 behind as a rear rank. Each front slot pairs with the
  nearest enemy across. Archers stay behind the rear rank (they already step back at 2 blocks).
- Attacks on players and monsters keep today's rules (surround, no cap on players), with the 1.0 to 1.6 band and the
  jitter fixes.
- `MaxAttackersPerTarget` 3 becomes 2 for NPC targets in line fights (rule 8); keep the config value for loose fights.
- Tests: deterministic battle sim in `tools/tests/factions` (two groups of 5 and 3 on flat ground, 30 s): no two NPCs
  closer than 0.8, every attacker's distance to its partner within 1.0 to 1.6 while swinging, facing changes under N
  per second, no enemy NPC with more than 2 attackers.
