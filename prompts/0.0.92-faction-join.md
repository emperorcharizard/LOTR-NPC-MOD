# 0.0.92 job script: players pick their faction

Built 2026-11-05 (second account, same chat). Factions 501 and spawn zones pass, DLL 0 warnings. **Not tested in game** (checklist section 92). Rebased at 0.0.92: the new base bundle holds this source; the patch starts empty.

## What the owner said, in order

1. "Something isnt working correctly in the config, the mod has been uploaded into the server, but when selecting a race with the lotr characters mod, it doesnt apply the player to the associated faction."
   - **Cause (by design since 0.0.35):** Gondor, Rohan, Dale and Arnor list no race, because LRC's one race `lrc:freepeople` fits all four. The orc factions and Isengard list none either (`lrc:orc` and `lrc:uruk`), because orc players were meant to join by group.
   - Haradrim, Dunlendings, Black Numenoreans, Easterlings, the dwarves, the Shire and the elves do map. Their codes match LRC 0.4.1.
2. "An alternate option, is when a fresh player spawns in they have a menu pop up asking what faction they intend to join. as well as a selectable menu command for all players. This can only be changed once (when they select the first time). (after that its changed by admins)"
3. "instead of applying the race allegiance, have them select right after making a character". So race no longer counts.
4. Groups: "A". They stop counting.
5. Which factions: "A". All of them, with a `Joinable` switch per faction.
6. Closing without a pick: "A". Allowed, and `/recruits join` reopens the menu until they pick.
7. "if they press escape without selecting a faction, give them a reminder of the command to open the menu in their local chat"
8. "Build all of it."

## What was built

- **Membership** (`FactionSystem.IsFriend`):
  - Only the assigned faction counts: the pick or an admin's `set`, both stored in `lotrnpcs:playerfactions`. Allies with ShareFriends still count, through `friendsFrom`.
  - The old race/group rule stays behind `RaceAndGroupMembership` (default false, top of the file). This is my call: it keeps that logic tested and gives admins a way back. The tests from before 0.0.92 run with it on (`SetLegacy` in the factions harness).
  - `FriendlyRaces` and `FriendlyGroups` stay in the file.
- **Config v21:** `Joinable` (per faction, default true) and `RaceAndGroupMembership` are new keys. There is nothing to migrate (they take their defaults).
  - #Instructions: a "Members (since 0.0.92)" line and a `RaceAndGroupMembership` line.
  - Texts: `set` and `clear` say "They can pick a faction again with /recruits join". `members` lists picks too. `check` shows "faction X". `faction list` leaves out races and groups while they don't count, and marks "not in the join menu".
- **Join menu** (`src/Joining.cs`, `JoinSystem`, channel `lotrnpcs-join`, messages `JoinMenu`, `JoinPick`, `JoinResult`):
  - **When it opens:** the server queues players without a faction on `PlayerNowPlaying`. It sends the menu once vanilla's `createCharacter` mod data is true, or 15 seconds after they joined (`WaitForCharacterSeconds`, for servers or character mods that don't set it). The client then waits until no character-creation dialog is open (class name contains CreateCharacter, CharacterCreat, CreateCustomCharacter or CharacterSelect).
  - **What it shows:** `JoinSystem.Options`, the factions with Joinable, in the creative-tab order, each with stance, follow limit and roster.
  - **The pick:** `JoinSystem.Pick` refuses a player who already has a faction, a faction that isn't Joinable, or an unknown one. Otherwise it sets and saves the faction and logs it.
  - **The dialog** (`GuiDialogJoinFaction`): two columns of faction buttons, then a confirmation ("Join the X? This is permanent"), then Join or Back. "Decide later" closes it. Closing without a pick shows `join-reminder` in the player's own chat (`capi.ShowChatMessage`). A repeat `/recruits join` while it's open keeps it open.
- **`/recruits join`** (players): opens the menu, or says "You are a member of the X. Only an admin can change your faction." Root help lists it.

## Calls I made (tell the owner)

- After an admin's `clear`, the player may pick again. The menu opens on their next join.
- The reminder appears when the menu closes without a pick. When a player without a faction joins, the menu itself opens (no extra chat line).
- `RaceAndGroupMembership` was kept as an off switch rather than deleting the old rule.

## Risks

- **Character mods:** the character-creation dialog check goes by class names. A character mod with a differently named dialog could have the menu open over it. The 15-second wait still helps.
- **Players who relied on race:** players whose faction came from their race (elves, dwarves, hobbits, Haradrim and so on) lose it until they pick. Players in group-based factions lose it too.
