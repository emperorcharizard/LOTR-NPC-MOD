# Job script: lotrnpcs 0.0.77 (as built)

Written 2026-11-04 from the owner's in-game test of 0.0.76 (screenshots and logs, 13:47 to 16:51) in the second
account's chat. Builds on the 0.0.76 base bundle (source hash `bd75c3ae97acca82`). **[owner]** = the owner's words or
decision, **[fact]** = checked in files or logs, **[default]** = my call, told to the owner, open to objection.

## 1. Two-handed holds never played

- **[owner]** "the dwarf greataxe isnt held in both hands"; "the dwarf battle axe is only held in one hand and kept at
  the side". The Mordor poleaxe orc in the owner's screenshot (Kragor) hangs its poleaxe the same way.
- **[fact]** server-debug.log: `lotrnpcs:test-dwarf-recruit attempted to play an animation code which its shape does not
  have: "poleaxehold"` (and `poleaxechop`, and the same for Mordor recruits and captains). The server builds an
  NPC's animations from its type's base shape, which was the game's `game:entity/humanoid/seraph-faceless`, never from
  the Player Model Lib race model. Our two animations live only in our bundled copies of the bodies, so the server
  refused them. This was the 0.0.68 risk: the poleaxe chop has never played in game.
- **Fix:** every NPC type's base shape is now `lotrnpcs:entity/humanoid/seraph-faceless` (gen `NPC_BODY`), our copy of
  the game body (same 20 elements as FA Races' copy of it, 238 animations = the game's 236 + ours). The creature item
  keeps the game's body. New factions test: every NPC type uses our body and every animation the type names is in it.

## 2. Follow and fight

- **[owner]** "for hired recruits in follow mode, make sure they prioritize attacking when in aggressive stance rather
  than trying to stay near me"; "when I stray to far, they run to me after they make a hit, then re aggro back onto the
  closest enemy"; "12 blocks, but when that amount is exceeded, they leave combat to return to you"; "Have it apply to
  all formations except loose, where on loose they dont try to return to me, and fight until the enemy is dead".
- **Cause [fact]:** after a blow the melee cooldown (about 2 s) plus the seek task's search wait leave a gap, and the
  follow task (checks every 0.4 s) started whenever the owner was more than 4 blocks away.
- **Built:**
  - `AiTaskFollowOwner.ShouldExecute`: a follower (not passive, not disengaging) that is fighting
    (`EntityBehaviorFactionMember.Fighting`, 3 s after its last chase, blow or hurt) doesn't start following.
  - Formation followers (order Follow, formation not loose, leader online): `HiringSystem.UpdateFollowFight` once a
    second. Targets more than `FollowFightRange` 12 blocks from the leader are not picked (`BeyondFollowRange`, in
    `RecruitWantsToAttack` and the seek task's `BeyondLeash`). The follower more than 12 from the leader breaks off
    (`StopFights`, now also the shoot task) and counts as disengaging (no fights, not even striking back) until within
    `RegroupWithin` 4 blocks **[default for the 4 and the no-strike-back]**.
  - Loose followers: no range limit; they come back when the fight is over; the 48-block teleport waits while a loose
    follower is fighting **[default]**.
  - Every stance but passive **[default]**. Hold and Guard keep their own rules.

## 3. Rathgul standing idle (attacker cap)

- **[owner]** "One of my npcs is just standing between me and the enemy not attacking. its set on aggressive";
  "rathgul is holding a knife though" (a melee recruit, not an archer).
- **[fact]** `MaxAttackersPerTarget` 3: a fourth NPC never picks a target three others fight.
- **[owner] chose a):** keep the cap, but when there is no other enemy within reach the extra NPCs join in.
  `AiTaskFactionSeek`: crowded candidates are passed over but the nearest is remembered (`NotCrowded`); if the search
  ran and found nothing else, that one becomes the target. Applies to every NPC, wild or hired. #Instructions and the
  config guide say so.

## 4. Archers shoot through friends

- **[owner]** "remove the hold fire rule". `AiTaskFactionShoot.ClearShot` only checks blocks now; our arrows already
  fly through the archer's friends (`LotrNpcsArrow.CanHitTarget`). `Archery.FriendInLine` kept for tests.
