LOTR Weapons

The weapon models and their textures were provided by the server's owner (the same models the LOTR NPCs mod's
NPCs carry). Parts without one of those textures use vanilla Vintage Story textures. The three animations of
our own (poleaxechop, poleaxehold, greatswordhold) were made for the LOTR NPCs mod from the game's own
axehit and holdbothhands.
