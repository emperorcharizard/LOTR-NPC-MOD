LOTR NPCs

The NPCs' race models come from LOTR Races and Classes by Dango Dango Dango, Shakgriig and Buggoi, a required mod.
The three orc models in assets/lotrnpcs/config/customplayermodels are made from LRC's own orc models and use its
shapes and textures; assets/lotrnpcs/patches/lrc-body-animations.json adds our weapon animations to LRC's bodies.

The Mordor and Misty Mountains orc weapon models in assets/lotrnpcs/shapes/item/orcweapon, and their textures in
assets/lotrnpcs/textures/item/orcweapon, were provided by the server's owner. Parts without one of those
textures use vanilla Vintage Story textures.
