LOTR NPCs

Race models, skins and textures for the NPCs come from LOTR Races and Classes
by Dango Dango Dango, Shakgriig and Buggoi, and are used with their permission.
They live in assets/lotrnpcs/config/customplayermodels, shapes/entity, shapes/seraphfaces, textures/entity and textures/icons.

The Mordor and Misty Mountains orc weapon models in assets/lotrnpcs/shapes/item/orcweapon, and their textures in
assets/lotrnpcs/textures/item/orcweapon, were provided by the server's owner. Parts without one of those
textures use vanilla Vintage Story textures.
